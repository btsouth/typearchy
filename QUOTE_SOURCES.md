# Quote sources

Quote Relay combines four short, attributed excerpts into one continuous test.
The active byline advances between excerpts while WPM and accuracy are measured
across the complete relay. Punctuation is normalized to plain keyboard
characters where needed.

must remain stable so personal-best comparisons stay meaningful. Avoid quotes
that are merely popular online without a traceable origin.
